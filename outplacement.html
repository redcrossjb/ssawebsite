How it works

Simple for you, personal for them

You're managing a hard moment for your organization. The program should make that easier, not add to it.

STEP 1

We scope it in one call

Tell me who's impacted and at what levels. We match each person to the right program, and I flex to your timeline, whether that's one person this month or a phased reduction.

STEP 2

Your people start fast

Each participant gets program access and books their first session with me directly. No intake queue, no handoffs. Delivery is virtual, and I work globally.

STEP 3

You pay for engagement, not seats

You're only invoiced when a person chooses to engage in the program. If someone already has their next role lined up and passes, you don't pay for a benefit nobody used.
