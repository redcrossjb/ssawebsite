About Jeff

I've been in the rooms where successor lists get made.

Most successors don't need more ambition. They need someone who has sat in the seat to tell them the truth about what's actually in the way.

I spent 13 years as a school teacher before I ever worked in HR. That's where I learned that people rarely lack ability. They lack clarity about what's holding them back.

From there I built a career in executive roles across private equity, publicly traded, and SMB companies, leading HR, legal, and strategy, and coaching CEOs and their leadership teams. I've sat in the succession conversations where names get weighed. I know what puts a leader on that list, and what quietly keeps one off it.

Six Summit Advisors is how I put that experience to work for the leaders coming up next. I use Hogan assessments and other psychometric tools to get past the fog fast, and I have a habit of connecting dots quickly so you can stop circling the problem and start moving.

When I need to think clearly, I go to Yellowstone. The best leadership lessons I know came from that landscape, and you'll hear a few of them if we work together.

The Six Summits Framework

The climb every leader makes

Every engagement connects to one or more of these six summits. For successors, the climb usually starts at Terrain.

SUMMIT 1

Terrain

Know the ground you're standing on. Honest self-inventory before you lead outward.

SUMMIT 2

Compass

Know where you're headed. Leadership that's intentional, not reactive.

SUMMIT 3

Pack

You don't summit alone. When the wolves came back to Yellowstone, they changed the rivers. Great leaders build other leaders.

SUMMIT 4

Altitude

The higher you go, the further you see. Influence across the whole enterprise.

SUMMIT 5

Weathered

Every leader gets a storm. Resilience through transitions and hard seasons.

SUMMIT 6

The View

What was the climb actually for? The legacy you leave in people.
